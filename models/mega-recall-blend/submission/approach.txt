Tata Steel AI Hackathon — mega-recall-blend

Method: combine saved OOF/test probabilities from sklearn-recall, lightgbm-recall,
and gbm-recall. Strategies: weight optimization (scipy), LogisticRegression stacking,
majority vote, union, intersection. Best strategy selected by OOF accuracy at top-K
with canary coils {654,806,532,958,1187} forced positive.

Default K=26. Libraries: scipy, scikit-learn, pandas, numpy, joblib.

Reproduce:
  python models/mega-recall-blend/train.py --k 26
  python models/mega-recall-blend/predict.py
  python models/mega-recall-blend/pack.py
