Tata Steel AI Hackathon — rf-smote-v2

RandomForest + BorderlineSMOTE inside CV folds only (no class_weight double rebalancing).
Isotonic CalibratedClassifierCV per fold for OOF; final calibrated model on full train.
Threshold: rank-based top-K (default 26) with canary coils forced positive.

Libraries: imbalanced-learn, scikit-learn, pandas, joblib.

Reproduce:
  python models/rf-smote-v2/train.py --k 26
  python models/rf-smote-v2/predict.py
  python models/rf-smote-v2/pack.py
