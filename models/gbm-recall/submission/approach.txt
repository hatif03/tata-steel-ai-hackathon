Tata Steel AI Hackathon — GBM Recall Ensemble
Method: models/gbm-recall

Equal-weight ensemble: XGBoost + LightGBM + CatBoost on utils/tabular_features.py.
Threshold: recall-first (100% OOF recall, else FPR<=3%, else t=0.05 per forum).

Unlike gbm-ensemble (failed LB 1.13208), this uses equal weights and low recall-oriented threshold.

Reproduce: python models/gbm-recall/train.py && predict.py && pack.py
