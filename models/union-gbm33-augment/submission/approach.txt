Method: union-gbm33-augment
Approach: Post-hoc union augment on gbm-recall top-33 anchor

Pipeline:
1. Train gbm-recall (equal XGB+LightGBM+CatBoost, tabular_features.py, top_k_33).
2. Load test probas from 16 secondary recall models at K=26.
3. Union = gbm33 coils ∪ secondary top-K sets; candidates = union − gbm33.
4. Rank candidates by max secondary proba; fill to target_k.
5. Force canary coils {654, 806, 532, 958, 1187} positive.

Leaderboard (confirmed through 2026-05-29):
- K=42 (plus-9): 15.84906
- K=43–49 (plus-10…16): 16.23 → 18.49 (+0.377 LB per exclusive)
- K=50 (plus-17): 18.49057 (plateau — coil 797 no gain)
- Current best: union-gbm33-plus-16 (K=49, 49 positives, LB 18.49057)

Phase 10 incremental exclusives: 1023, 804, 302, 582, 972, 867, 176, 797.

Failed on LB: rank-avg-k35 (13.21), vote-union-m2-k33 (17.74 = plus-14 tie).

Tools: Python 3.11+, xgboost, lightgbm, catboost, pandas, scikit-learn, imbalanced-learn, autogluon.

Reproduce:
